# Kingdom Connect glossary

The words this project uses, and the ones it avoids. Definitions only; requirements live in [docs/prd.md](../docs/prd.md).

**Member**: a person with an approved account (Verified Member, Level 1 or higher). _Avoid:_ user (too broad), church member (Plan wording; anyone in the church, not only those on the platform).

**Applicant**: someone who has registered but is not yet approved. Cannot see member content.

**Verified Business (Level 2)**: a member whose business documents (BRELA, TRA TIN, licence) are approved. Listed in the Business directory. _Avoid:_ business owner, for this level; a business owner may still be Level 1.

**Opportunity Poster (Level 3)**: a Verified Business whose directorship or authorisation and address are approved. Can post Opportunities.

**Opportunity**: a post members can respond to. Types: Job, Training/Internship, Partnership, Collaboration, Investment, Tender. Posted by an Opportunity Poster or an Administrator.

**Tender**: a type of Opportunity (decided 15 Oct 2026), not a separate module. Members express interest rather than apply. Exact fields: see ticket "Tenders as an Opportunity type".

**Investor flag**: a private permission on a member account that adds anonymous interest, silent browsing, investor filters and a saved list. Never shown to members. _Avoid:_ investor, as a role name or category shown in the UI. Any member can invest without the flag.

**Idea**: an entry in the ideas pool with a public summary and confidential details.

**Idea access request**: a request to see an Idea's details, named or anonymous, which the owner approves or declines.

**Administrator**: platform staff who verify, moderate and publish. Does not connect or message. _Avoid:_ Church Admin (old Osborn project term).
