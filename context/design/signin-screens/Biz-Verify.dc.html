What each level shows

Each level shows its status: Not started, Pending, Approved, Rejected (with the reason) or Expired.While a level is Pending, its form is closed. If it is rejected, you see the reason and the form again with your earlier details.

Your documents are kept in a store only staff can open. Members never see them.
