Full description

Families rent a full uniform set for the school year and return it in December. Returned sets are cleaned, mended and rented again, or sold at a low price.

Business model

A yearly rental fee per set, paid in two parts. Resale of sets that can no longer be rented.

Market

Primary and secondary schools in Kinondoni and Ubungo. We start with 4 schools that already know our tailoring work.
