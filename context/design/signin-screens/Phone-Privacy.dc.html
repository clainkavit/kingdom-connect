Privacy notice and data consent

Draft · version 0.1 · 2 Nov 2026

This notice explains what personal data Kingdom Connect collects through Kingdom Connect, why, who can see it and what your rights are under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. Kingdom Connect is the data controller.

What we collect

  • Your name, username, email address, +255 phone number and Tanzanian address.
  • Your ID type and number, a photo of your ID and a photo of your face. The ID number is stored encrypted.
  • Your profile: category, profession, skills, sector, study details and business details if you add them.
  • What you do on Kingdom Connect: connections, messages, posts, responses, ideas, plans, reports and the legal texts you accepted, with the time and the internet (IP) address used.

Why we use it

  • To check that you are who you say you are, and that one person has one account.
  • To run Kingdom Connect: your profile, connections, messages, opportunities, ideas, training and events.
  • To suggest people and opportunities that match your skills, sector and location.
  • To keep members safe: to review reports, stop scams and keep a record of administrators' actions.
  • To send you sign-in codes and the notifications you choose.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Other members see your profile. They never see your phone number or email unless you are connected and you have switched sharing on for that detail. [TO CONFIRM: whether your address can also be shared this way]
  • Administrators see what they need to verify you and handle reports. Your ID photo and face photo are kept in a separate store that only administrators can open. Viewing your full ID number is logged.
  • Service providers that host Kingdom Connect and send our emails process data for us under contract. [TO CONFIRM: hosting provider and the country where data is stored]
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your data, we remove your profile, contact details, ID photos and files. Your account stays as "Deleted member" so that other people's conversations, responses and milestone plans still make sense. [TO CONFIRM: what else is kept after deletion, and for how long] Our audit log of administrators' actions is kept for at least 5 years. [TO CONFIRM: how long ID photos are kept after a decision]

Your rights

You can see and download your data, correct it, ask us to delete it, and withdraw this consent, from Settings or by contacting us. [TO CONFIRM: how consent is withdrawn and what happens to the account] You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

Your consent

By ticking the box at registration, you agree that Kingdom Connect may collect and use your personal data as this notice describes. This consent is separate from the terms of use.

Changes

When we change this notice, we show you the new version at your next sign-in and ask you to accept it. If the English and Kiswahili versions differ, the English version governs.
