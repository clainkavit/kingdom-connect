  2. Product overview and goals
  Kingdom Connect is a verified, members-only platform where the Mkuyuni Community
  finds people, opportunities and ideas to grow together, on web and mobile.

  Problem. Members meet at church but have no way to find each other's skills, businesses,
  ideas or opportunities during the week. Opportunities get missed, and unverified
  channels expose members to job and investment scams.

  Product goals



                                                                                       Page 1 of 21

   1. Every member can find verified professionals and businesses by skill, sector and
      location.
   2. Members can find and apply for safe, verified opportunities, with scam posts removed
      fast.
   3. Innovators can share ideas and meet investors or partners without losing control of
      their IP.
   4. Investors can find ideas discreetly, without being publicly listed or pitched.
   5. Members can grow through training, events, tenders and community news.

  Non-goals (Phase 1)

       Moving money: no payments, course fees, escrow or investment transactions.
       Mentorship matching.
       Members resident outside Tanzania.
       Public, non-member access to any content beyond the landing and sign-up pages.

  6. Registration, login and verification
  No one reaches member content until an administrator has verified their NIDA identity,
  and business rights unlock one level at a time.

  Stories

          REG-3 As an admin, I want to review pending profiles and approve or reject them so
          that only real members get in. (FR-03, UC-07–08)
          REG-4 As a member, I want to log in with password and OTP so that my account is

  Acceptance criteria

   1. The wizard collects full name, username, address, NIDA number, email, phone,
      occupation category (Professional, Businessperson, Entrepreneur, Other) and optional
      study info.
   2. Category-specific fields appear based on the category (for example, CPA number for
      auditors; CISSP, CISM, CEH for cybersecurity).
   3. NIDA number must be 20 digits and unique across accounts; a duplicate blocks
       submission and alerts admins (duplicate-profile detection). In Phase 1 an admin checks
       NIDA details manually; integration with NIDA's verification service may follow if
       problems arise.
   4. On submit, status = Pending and the applicant receives an email and SMS confirmation.
   5. Admin sees each pending applicant with documents side by side and must give a
      reason when rejecting; the reason is sent to the applicant.
   6. Status values: Pending → Approved or Rejected. A rejected applicant can correct and
      resubmit once (Proposed).
   7. Login requires username/password, then a 6-digit OTP sent by email during the pilot
      (SMS OTP considered later, depending on cost), valid 5 minutes, 3 attempts (Proposed
       values).
   8. Five failed password attempts lock the account for 15 minutes (Proposed).

  9. Connections and messaging
  Two members can message only after one accepts the other's connection request. (FR-
  13–14, UC-14–16)

  Stories




                                                                                                Page 10 of 21
Kingdom Connect – Product Requirements Document (PRD)




       CON-1 As a member, I want to send a connection request with a short note so that the
       other person knows why I'm reaching out.
       CON-2 As a member, I want to accept or decline requests so that I control who can
       message me.
       CON-3 As a connected member, I want to message securely so that I don't need to
       share my phone number.

  Acceptance criteria

   1. Request states: Sent → Accepted, Declined or Withdrawn. Declining does not notify
       the sender (Proposed: the request simply stays pending for them for 30 days, then
       expires).
   2. The request note is optional, up to 300 characters.
   3. A member can send at most 30 requests a day to slow spam (Proposed).
   4. Messages support text and attachments (PDF, images, up to 10 MB). Messages are
      encrypted in transit and at rest.
   5. Either side can remove a connection or block a member; blocking stops messages and
      hides each from the other's searches.
   6. Every message thread has a "Report" action that sends the thread to admins (section
      13).
   7. Idea-related conversations (section 11) open as message threads too, labelled with the
      idea title.

  13. Reports, safety and administration
  A reported opportunity disappears from members' view the moment it is reported;
  admins then restore it or act on it, and every admin action is logged. (FR-26–29, FR-35–36,
  FR-49, UC-32–34)

  Stories

       SAFE-1 As a member, I want to report a scam or bad user from any profile, opportunity,
       idea or message thread.
       SAFE-2 As an admin, I want one queue of reports, pending posts and re-verifications so
       that nothing is missed.
       SAFE-3 As an admin, I want to suspend repeat offenders.
       SAFE-4 As a Super Admin, I want an audit log of every admin and sensitive action.

  Acceptance criteria

   1. The report form asks for a reason (Asked for money, Fake job or business, Harassment,
      Impersonation, Spam, Other) and an optional note and screenshot.
   2. Reporting an opportunity or tender sets it to Hidden immediately. Profiles and ideas
      stay visible until an admin reviews them.
   3. The reporter gets a reference number and the final outcome (Action taken / No
      action), not the details.
   4. Admin actions: Restore, Edit request to poster, Remove, Warn, Suspend posting rights,
      Suspend account. Each requires a note.
   5. Two upheld reports against a business within 12 months suspend its posting rights;
      three suspend the account pending review.
   6. Re-verification: 30 and 7 days before a business licence expires, the member is
      reminded. On expiry, Level 2 and 3 rights pause until new documents are approved.
   7. Audit log records who, what, when, and before/after values for approvals, rejections,
       moderation, role changes, investor flag changes, and every view of idea details. Logs
       are read-only and kept at least 5 years (Proposed).
   8. Admins cannot see message contents unless a thread has been reported.

  15. Non-functional requirements

     Area                             Requirement

                                      public URLs

     Performance                      Pages and searches load in under 2 seconds on 3G
                                      for 95% of requests; app usable on Android 9+ and
                                      iOS 15+
