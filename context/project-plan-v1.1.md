**SUMMARY OF CHANGES IN VERSION 1.1**

- **Name and tagline:** the platform continues as Kingdom Connect, with the tagline “People • Opportunities • Growth”.

- **Dashboards:** separate Member and Administrator dashboards. Verification and moderation figures appear only on the Administrator dashboard.

- **Business verification:** tiered verification. Only Level 3 (Opportunity Poster) businesses can post opportunities, with probation, mandatory posting fields, annual re-verification and fast takedown.

- **Investors:** investor status is a private permission rather than a public category. There is no investor directory, and identities are revealed only on mutual consent.

- **Ideas Pool:** replaces the Innovation & Investment Hub. Ideas are linked to the owner’s profile and use two-layer visibility (summary and confidential details).

- **Expanded Phase 1:** Training & Courses, Events, Tenders, News & Updates, Featured Opportunities and Scan for Opportunities are now in scope.

- **Removed:** the mentorship network (former FR-24) and the separate student category. Study information is now an optional field on any profile.

- **Geography:** Phase 1 is limited to Tanzania with NIDA-based verification. Members outside Tanzania are a future enhancement.

- **Timeline:** extended from 9 to 12 months to reflect the expanded scope.

**1. PROJECT OVERVIEW**

The Kingdom Connect Platform (People • Opportunities • Growth) is a digital transformation initiative aimed at creating a secure and trusted networking ecosystem for the Mkuyuni Community. The platform will connect professionals, entrepreneurs, business owners, investors, and innovators, enabling them to collaborate, share opportunities, build partnerships, and support one another’s growth.

The project seeks to address the current lack of visibility and connectivity among community members by providing a centralised platform where individuals can showcase their professional expertise, promote businesses, post and discover opportunities, share ideas in a common ideas pool, access training and events, and explore investment opportunities. Through verified membership, tiered business verification, strong governance, and robust security controls, Kingdom Connect will foster trust, encourage collaboration, and drive economic empowerment within the community.

Phase 1 will serve members resident in Tanzania, with identity verification based on NIDA. Expansion to members outside Tanzania is planned for a later phase.

Ultimately, the platform will serve as a catalyst for professional development, entrepreneurship, business growth, innovation, and sustainable community impact, while reinforcing the shared values and vision of the Mkuyuni Community.

**2. BACKGROUND**

For many years, God has been teaching and guiding His people through the ministry of Pastor Tony, providing practical and godly principles for living, working, and interacting with the world while remaining faithful to God’s ways. Through these teachings, many young people within the community have been equipped with spiritual wisdom, strong moral values, professional integrity, and a purpose-driven mindset.

As a result, this community has produced individuals whom employers would be eager to hire, business owners would be willing to partner with, and investors would be interested in supporting. Within the community are professionals, entrepreneurs, innovators, and individuals with impactful ideas that, with the right connections and support, have the potential to create positive change both locally and globally.

Despite the strength and value of this network, there is currently no dedicated platform that allows members of the community to connect beyond church services. While members gather regularly for worship and fellowship, they often return to their daily activities with limited opportunities to identify, engage, and collaborate with others who share similar interests, skills, business ambitions, or investment opportunities.

This project seeks to address that challenge by creating a platform where community members can build professional profiles, showcase their skills, businesses, expertise, and ideas, and connect with like-minded individuals. The platform will enable networking, collaboration, business partnerships, employment opportunities, training, and investment connections, fostering a stronger and more impactful community that extends beyond the church walls.

**3. PROBLEM STATEMENT**

Despite the strong network of professionals, entrepreneurs, innovators, and investors nurtured through years of godly teaching by Pastor Tony, there is no dedicated platform that enables community members to connect, collaborate, and support one another beyond church services.

As a result:

- Professional and business opportunities are often missed.

- Talented individuals and businesses lack visibility.

- Entrepreneurs and innovators struggle to find partners and investors.

- Professional networks remain fragmented and underutilised.

- Meaningful collaboration within the community is limited.

Therefore, there is a need for a platform that connects members, promotes opportunities, and facilitates professional, business, and economic growth within the community.

**11. BUSINESS VERIFICATION AND OPPORTUNITY POSTING CONTROLS**

Because businesses can post opportunities, business verification is stricter than professional verification. Verification is tiered so that only the most thoroughly verified businesses can post.

| **Level** | **What It Allows** | **Requirements** |
|----|----|----|
| 1\. Verified Member | Normal member access | NIDA verification and manual review |
| 2\. Verified Business | Business listed in the Business Directory; may apply for investor access | Level 1, plus BRELA registration certificate, TRA TIN certificate and a valid business licence |
| 3\. Opportunity Poster | Can post opportunities and tenders | Level 2, plus proof that the person is a director or authorised representative (BRELA records or an authorisation letter), and a confirmed physical address |

All businesses must be formally registered with BRELA to be listed in Phase 1.

**Posting Controls**

- **Probation period:** a new Opportunity Poster’s first few opportunities go through administrator review before going live. After a clean record, they post directly.

- **Mandatory fields:** every posting must include role, location, a contact channel through the platform, and a declaration that no fees are charged to applicants.

- **Annual re-verification:** businesses re-submit current documents each year, since business licences expire.

- **Fast takedown:** reported opportunities are hidden immediately pending review, and repeat offences suspend the business.

**12. INVESTOR ACCESS MODEL**

Investors are kept low-key. They exist on the platform and can act, but members cannot browse or count them.

**Principles**

- **Hidden investor flag:** an investor’s public profile shows only their profession or business. Investor status is a private permission, not a public label.

- **No investor directory:** there is no investor list and no investor count on the Member dashboard.

- **Anonymous interest:** idea owners see “a verified investor” with sector and ticket size. Names are revealed only when the owner approves and the investor accepts the NDA.

- **Silent browsing:** investor views of idea summaries are logged for audit and shown to owners only as a count.

- **Admin accountability:** administrators can see the full investor list, verification records and access logs.

**Routes to Investing**

The investor flag provides discretion and tools, not exclusive permission to invest. Any verified member can invest in or partner on an idea.

| **Route** | **Who** | **How It Works** | **What They Get** |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1\. Member interest | Any verified member, including business owners | Clicks “I’m interested” and chooses Invest, Partner or Both. The request shows their name and profile. | Same idea-access flow: owner approval, NDA, details, messaging |
| 2\. Investor flag | Invited investors, or Verified Business owners who apply | Invitation by leadership or an administrator, or an application reviewed by an administrator | Anonymous interest, silent browsing, investment filters, private saved list |

The platform does not process money in Phase 1. Both routes end with an agreement between the parties (UC-24 to UC-27).

**13. IDEAS POOL VISIBILITY MODEL**

Each idea in the pool has two layers of visibility.

| **Layer** | **Who Sees It** | **What Is Shown** |
|----|----|----|
| Summary card | All verified members | Title, problem being solved, industry, stage, what is sought (investor, partner or both), optional investment range, and the owner’s name linked to their profile (unless posted without name) |
| Full details | Only people approved by the owner | Full description, business model, financials, team and attached documents. Documents are watermarked and every view is logged. |

**Viewing Flow**

1.  A member opens Ideas in the sidebar, or the Ideas tab on a member’s profile.

2.  They browse or filter summary cards and open one to read the summary.

3.  They click “Request details” or “I’m interested”, and the owner is notified.

4.  The owner approves or declines. Requests from flagged investors show as “a verified investor” with sector and ticket size.

5.  The requester accepts the NDA and IP terms. Both identities are revealed, the details unlock, and messaging opens.

6.  The owner can view everyone with access and revoke access at any time.

**Owner Controls and Moderation**

- Each idea can be published, published without name, or kept as a draft visible only to the owner.

- New ideas pass administrator moderation before appearing in the pool, to filter out spam and fraudulent investment schemes.

| Idea Theft | Intellectual property loss | NDA, owner-approved access, watermarking and logging |
| Investor Exposure | Unwanted solicitation, investor withdrawal | Private investor flag, anonymous interest, no investor directory |
| Insider Misuse | Abuse of privileges | Audit logging |
| Expanded Scope | Schedule and budget overrun | Two development releases and a 12-month timeline |
| Low User Adoption | Reduced value realisation | Awareness campaigns and training |
