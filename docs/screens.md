Every screen the product needs, who can open it, what it shows, and the states that differ from the normal case. It replaces the 16-screen list in PRD section 5, which predates several decisions (milestone plans, the nationwide sign-up, legal texts and the notification centre).

## How to read it

**Roles.** Visitor (signed out), Signed in (got in with Google, Facebook, Apple, a phone number or email and accepted the terms, but has not sent the verification steps; added 14 Nov 2026), Applicant (sent the verification steps, not yet approved), Member (Level 1 and up), Business (Level 2), Poster (Level 3), Investor (a member with the private flag), Admin, Super Admin. Rights stack as in PRD section 3.

**Screen IDs.** `S` is a member-side screen, `A` is an admin-side screen. They are the reference for the build tickets and the access-rule tests.

### Five states every screen is checked for

| State | What the screen does |
|---|---|
| Loading | Shows a skeleton of the final layout, not a blank page or a spinner alone. Lists show skeleton rows. |
| Empty | Says what will appear here and how to add the first item (for example "No connection requests yet. Find people to connect with."). Never a blank area. |
| Error | Says what failed in plain words, offers Try again, and keeps anything the person typed. No technical codes. |
| Locked for this role | A signed-in member who lacks the level sees the screen's name, one line of reason and the path forward (for example "Verify your business to respond"). Screens that must not reveal that they exist (admin screens, investor tools) show nothing at all to people without them, as if they were not there. |
| Slow or offline | The PRD targets 3G. A screen keeps showing what it already loaded, shows a small "Slow connection" banner, queues sends (a message, a request) and retries, and never shows an empty state because the network failed. |

The tables below list only a screen's **special states**: the ones beyond these five.

### Everywhere

- **Languages.** Every screen is English and Kiswahili, in both apps. Visitors switch from the sign-in header or the landing footer; signed-in members from Settings. Until Settings is built, the switch is in the member top bar, the phone More screen and the admin sidebar. A Member's choice is saved to their account; everyone else's to the device.
- **Computer top bar (13 Nov 2026, R22 #245).** A light bar: the platform mark and name, a search box in the middle, the five sections with an icon above each word and an underline on the current one, a count of unread conversations on Messages (1 to 9, then 9+; also on the phone tab, R24), then the bell, a globe button with the language code (opens English / Kiswahili) and the person icon. The search box opens `/search?q=`: People (the directory search, first 5, "See all in People" opens `/people?q=`), Opportunities (`search_opportunities`: open posts whose title, role, description, poster or skills hold every word, up to 20) and Training (`search_trainings`: published, upcoming training whose title, provider, details or venue hold every word). Both match on the server (R23 #247); under 2 letters nothing is searched.
- **Phone layout.** The menu becomes a bottom tab bar (member: Home, People, Opportunities, Growth, Messages, each icon with its word, the same five as the computer top bar, decided 12 Nov 2026; admin: Home, Verify, Moderate, Reports, More). The person icon opens My account (`/account`): My profile, News and updates, Reports & Safety, Settings, language and Log out. Growth (`/growth`) lists Ideas, Training, Events and Milestone plans. Tables become cards and filters open in a sheet.
- **The investor flag is never visible.** An investor's menu looks the same as any member's. Investor tools (filters, Saved ideas, anonymous interest) appear inside Ideas only, and are absent, not locked, for everyone else.
- **Report.** Profiles, opportunities, ideas and message threads each carry a Report action that opens S42.
- **Look, but not act (14 Nov 2026, PRD section 3).** Signed in and Applicant reach Home, Opportunities (list and detail), Growth (idea summaries, training, events) and News, read only. People, every profile, Messages and Connections are locked. Every action that reaches another member (Apply, Express interest, Connect, Message, Register, Post, Save, Rate, I'm interested) opens "Verify your identity to ..." with Continue verification (S05) and Not now. The server refuses those actions; the app only explains.
- **Design (14 Nov 2026).** Every screen follows the [combined design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/JvqFyq1vGV2PPtQjQBmWWC), whose board titles carry these screen IDs. Built screens drawn to the earlier canvas are out of date until rebuilt to it: S08, S10, S11, S16 to S21, S22 to S29, S40, A01 to A03 and A17. Each gets a "match the combined canvas" build issue. Saved opportunities and job alerts (S16) are on it unchanged.
