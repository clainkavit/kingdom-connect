# Milestone plan disclaimer

Both members accept this when they start a milestone plan on {platform_name}.

## What a milestone plan is

A milestone plan is a shared record of the steps two members agree to take together, for an idea or an opportunity. The member responsible for a milestone marks it done, and the other member confirms it. Either member can upload a signed agreement.

## What {entity_name} does

- Keeps the record: the stages, the dates, who confirmed what, and any signed document you upload.
- Lets administrators see the signed document only while a report about the plan is open.

## What {entity_name} does not do

- It is not a party to your agreement or your work together.
- It does not check, approve or guarantee the other member's promises, work, money or results.
- It does not hold, send or receive any money.
- It does not settle disputes between you. The record may help you or a court, but deciding a dispute is for you, a mediator or a court.

## Your responsibility

- You decide whether to work with the other member, and on what terms.
- Get your own legal, financial or tax advice where you need it.
- Put anything important in a written agreement signed by both of you.

## Ending a plan

While a plan is waiting for the other member, the member who proposed it can withdraw it. Once both members have accepted the first stage, either can withdraw within 1 hour. After that hour, either member can end the plan with a reason. The plan stays on record as ended, with all its milestones. Nothing is deleted.

## Law

This disclaimer is governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania. If the English and Kiswahili versions differ, the English version governs.
