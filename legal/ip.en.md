You accept these terms together with the non-disclosure agreement, when you ask to see the details of another member's idea. They are between you and the idea's owner. {entity_name} is not a party to them.

## The owner keeps the idea

- The idea, its details, files and any designs, names or know-how in it belong to the owner, or to whoever the owner got them from.
- Seeing the details gives you no right to use, copy, sell, register or build on the idea.
- Any right to use the idea must be agreed with the owner, in writing, outside these terms.

## Your own ideas

These terms do not take away anything you already owned before you saw the details, or anything you create later on your own without using the owner's confidential information.

## Registering rights

You agree not to register a patent, trademark, business name or design based on the owner's idea, and not to help anyone else do so.

## Posting an idea

When you post your own idea on {platform_name}, you keep all rights to it. You allow {platform_name} to show its summary to members, and its details only to members you approve.
