# Non-disclosure agreement

This agreement is between the member who owns the idea (the owner) and the member who asked to see its details (you). You accept it on {platform_name} after the owner approves your request, and before the details open. {entity_name} records your acceptance but is not a party to this agreement.

## Confidential information

The details of the idea, and every file, figure, plan or explanation the owner shares with you about it, are confidential information. The public summary that every member can already see is not.

## What you agree to

- Use the confidential information only to decide whether to invest, partner or work with the owner on this idea.
- Not share it with anyone, unless the owner agrees in writing. If you need to share it with your own adviser, that adviser must keep it confidential too, and you remain responsible.
- Not copy, download, photograph or record the details, except where {platform_name} lets you do so.
- Not use it to build, fund or help a competing idea.
- Stop using it, and delete any copies you have, when the owner asks or when your access ends.

## What this does not cover

Information you can show you already had, that was already public, or that someone gave you without breaking a duty of confidence.
