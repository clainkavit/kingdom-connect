# Privacy notice and data consent

This notice explains what personal data {entity_name} collects through {platform_name}, why, who can see it and what your rights are under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. {entity_name} is the data controller.

## What we collect

- Your name, username, email address, +255 phone number and Tanzanian address.
- Your ID type and number, a photo of your ID and a photo of your face. The ID number is stored encrypted.
- Your profile: category, profession, skills, sector, study details and business details if you add them.
- What you do on {platform_name}: connections, messages, posts, responses, ideas, plans, reports and the legal texts you accepted, with the time and the internet (IP) address used.

## Why we use it

- To check that you are who you say you are, and that one person has one account.
- To run {platform_name}: your profile, connections, messages, opportunities, ideas, training and events.
- To suggest people and opportunities that match your skills, sector and location.
- To keep members safe: to review reports, stop scams and keep a record of administrators' actions.
- To send you sign-in codes and the notifications you choose.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Other members** see your profile. They never see your phone number, email or address unless you are connected and you have switched sharing on for that detail.
- **Administrators** see what they need to verify you and handle reports. Your ID photo and face photo are kept in a separate store that only administrators can open. Viewing your full ID number is logged.

- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your data, we remove your profile, contact details, ID photos and files. Your account stays as "Deleted member" so that other people's conversations, responses and milestone plans still make sense. ## Your rights

You can see and download your data, correct it, ask us to delete it, and withdraw this consent, from Settings or by contacting us. If you withdraw this consent, your account is closed, because we cannot verify you or run your account without processing your data. You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

## Your consent

By ticking the box at registration, you agree that {entity_name} may collect and use your personal data as this notice describes. This consent is separate from the terms of use.

## Changes

When we change this notice, we show you the new version at your next sign-in and ask you to accept it. If the English and Kiswahili versions differ, the English version governs.
